Privacy Policy

POSEIDON Backups · Vivito IT Solutions · Effective October 2, 2026

POSEIDON Backups is the Google Drive connection used by the server of POSEIDON, a water utility management system developed by Vivito IT Solutions, to store off-site copies of its automatic database backups. This policy explains what it accesses and why.

What it accesses

  • Only the files it creates. It uses Google Drive's limited drive.file permission, so it can create and update its own backup files and folder, and cannot see, read or change any other file in the Google Drive account.
  • No profile data is collected. It does not read your contacts, email, calendar or any other Google data.

What it stores

Backup files of a POSEIDON database, uploaded from the utility's own server to Vivito IT Solutions' Google Drive. These files contain the water utility's records — such as consumer accounts, billing and payments — and are kept on behalf of the local government unit that operates POSEIDON, for disaster recovery only.

How it is used and shared

  • The files are used only to restore POSEIDON if its server's data is lost or damaged.
  • They are not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone other than the water utility they belong to, except where required by law.
  • Access to the Google account that holds them is limited to Vivito IT Solutions and protected with 2-step verification.

Retention and deletion

Each new backup replaces the previous "latest" file, and dated daily copies are kept for a limited period (7 days by default) and then removed. The utility may ask for its backups to be deleted at any time. Access can also be revoked at any time from the Google Account's third-party access settings.

Google API Services

POSEIDON Backups' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data Privacy Act

Vivito IT Solutions handles this data as a personal information processor for the local government unit, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). Residents' requests about their own data should be made to their water utility, the personal information controller.
